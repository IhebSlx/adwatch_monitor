# AdWatch — what is in it, where it came from, and why it sits where it sits

Written 2026-08-10. Every number below was measured against `data/adwatch.db`
(130,9 MB) on that date, not recalled. Re-measure before quoting any of it later.

Companion documents: `DATA-QUALITY.md` (what was cleaned and how to read the
numbers), `ONBOARDING.md` (how a colleague starts using it).

---

## 0. The one-paragraph version

AdWatch joins three things that have never been in one place: **Solarlux CRM
master data and history** (46k+ Firmen, 58k Verkaufschancen, 92k order events),
**what those companies say about themselves on their own websites** (crawled and
extracted), and **whether they are currently buying advertising** (Meta ad
library). The CRM knows who bought. The website knows what kind of company they
are. The ad data knows whether they are still spending money in the market — and
that last signal exists nowhere else in the Solarlux stack. The app is built so
that a company that stopped buying from us but is visibly still advertising
surfaces to the top of a call list.

---

## 1. Where every piece of data comes from

There are exactly **three** origins. Everything in the app is one of these.

### A. Dataverse / Dynamics CRM — pulled directly, read-only

Pulled through the browser session (`https://slxcrowd.crm4.dynamics.com/api/data/v9.2/`),
paginated with `Prefer: odata.maxpagesize=5000` + `@odata.nextLink`. **Never
written to.**

| CRM table | lands in | rows today |
|---|---|---|
| `account` | `companies` | 47.770 with a `crm_id` |
| `opportunity` | `crm_opportunities` | 57.776 |
| `slx_product` (opportunity link) | `crm_opportunity_products` | 145.865 |
| `slx_product` (account link) | `crm_company_products` | 38.430 |
| Beleg export → order events | `crm_order_events` | 91.992 |

### B. Excel / CSV uploads — the historical exports

The Beleg (invoice document) export and the original monitored-company list came
in as files. Belege were collapsed into **order events** (one company + one day =
one event), because a big dealer issues several documents per order and the raw
cadence reads as 0–3 days. `revenue_y0..y4` on `companies` also came from an
export and is thin: only **1.048 / 1.903 / 1.920 / 860** companies have values.
That is why the Chancen tab uses order events, not those columns.

### C. Generated by AdWatch itself — this is the part that does not exist in CRM

| what | table / column | rows today |
|---|---|---|
| Meta ad pages resolved per company | `company_pages` | 783 |
| Individual ads seen | `ads` | 1.381 |
| Weekly ad activity per company | `weekly_company_metrics` | 358 rows, 226 companies, 2026-07-06 → 2026-08-03 |
| Website found + verified as really theirs | `companies.website_domain`, `identity_status` | 24.402 domains |
| Facts extracted from the website by Haiku | `company_enrichment` + ~25 `companies` columns | 1.781 records, 1.447 companies |
| ICP profiles + scores | `icp_profiles`, `fit_score`, `target_score` | 10 profiles |
| Health / Rhythmus / win-back rank | `companies.health`, `winback_score` | all |

## 3. The tabs, one by one

The tabs are not a menu of views over one table. **Each tab is a different unit
of analysis**, and that is the answer to "why is this separate":

| Tab | one row = |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | one company, seen through *ad activity this week* |
| Companies | one **Firma** (the master record) |
| Rückgewinnung | one **buying relationship over time** |
| Objekte | one **Bauvorhaben** — not a company at all |
| Prüfen | one **decision waiting for a human** |
| ICP | the **model**, not data |

Merging any two of them would mean putting two different grains in one table.
That is exactly the mistake that produced "34 von 300 Zeilen" earlier.

---

### 3.1 Dashboard — the ad signal

**Data:** `weekly_company_metrics`, `ads`, `company_pages` (AdWatch-generated),
joined to `companies` for revenue.

Shows *Interessante Partner*, ranked by **Divergenz = M × G**:

- **M (0–100)** Marketing-Aktivität — active ads, campaign recency, momentum, platform breadth
- **G (0–1)** Umsatz-Lücke — `lapsed` 1.0 · `steep` 0.8 · `never` 0.6 · `mild` 0.4 · `healthy` 0.15

A partner who stopped buying but runs fresh campaigns is alive and investing in
lead generation — just not with us. Companies never fetched get **no** score
("unbewertet"); unknown must never look like inactive.

**Honest limit:** 226 companies have weekly metrics and 671 have a Meta page, out
of 48.239. This tab covers a sliver of the base, and that sliver was picked from
the old buyer list — see the leakage warning in §4.

---

### 3.2 Companies — the master record and the Explorer

**Data:** everything on the `companies` row. That is CRM master data (SAP-Nummer,
KV, Segment, Untersegment, Vertriebsweg, Adresse), plus the AdWatch-generated
website columns, plus the stored scores.

Clicking a row opens the dossier: CRM fields, the enrichment (extracted facts +
judgements), the product profile, the Verkaufschancen this firm is on, health,
and scores. This is the **entity view** — everything true about one company.

**Website columns and where each came from:**

```
website_source:   crm 22.714 · marktanalyse 269 · serper 52 · manual 1
identity_status:  unverified 22.794 · verified 1.541 · not_found 129
                  needs_review 38 · unreachable 33 · conflict 9 · (leer) 23.695
enrichment:       enriched 1.447 · error 154 · needs_review 36 · no_website_found 27
```

A domain from CRM is *claimed*, not *proven*. `verified` means hard evidence was
found on the page itself (phone, or PLZ+Straße, or PLZ+Name).

### 3.3 Rückgewinnung ("Chancen") — and what **Rhythmus** means

**These are not Verkaufschancen from CRM.** The tab is named after the business
opportunity, not the CRM object. It is computed purely from `crm_order_events`.

**Rhythmus = the company's own median number of days between order events.**

Concretely, per company:

1. Every Beleg is collapsed to one event per **company + day** (73.112 Belege →
   ~54.534 events; several documents per order otherwise read as a 0–3 day cadence).
2. All gaps between consecutive event days are taken, and the **median** is the
   Rhythmus (`cadence_days`, shown as "142 T").
3. It needs **at least 4 events** — with fewer there is no rhythm and the app
   falls back to absolute age (365 days).

The other columns follow from it:

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rhythmus** | median days between this company's own orders |
| **still seit** | days since its last order |
| **überfällig** | `still seit ÷ Rhythmus` — how many of *its own* intervals have passed |

**Thresholds** (`adwatch/insights/rfm.py`):

```
gefährdet   überfällig ≥ 3× AND still seit ≥ 120 Tage
verloren    überfällig ≥ 6×
beobachten  überfällig ≥ 1,5× AND still seit ≥ 60 Tage
einmalig    bought, but never anything ≥ 2.000 € — spare parts only
```

**Why a relative measure and not "12 Monate ohne Bestellung":** the cadences in
this base run from a few days (Bauelementehandel ordering constantly) to a full
year (Wohnungswirtschaft doing one refurbishment). A fixed rule either screams at
the first or never notices the second. 120 days is the floor so a fortnightly
orderer does not alarm every month.

Current distribution: `nie` 40.036 · `einmalig` 5.147 · `aktiv` 1.244 ·
`verloren` 749 · `gefährdet` 313 · `beobachten` 281.

**Why this is its own tab:** it is a *worklist*, ordered by `winback_score`
(value × how clearly they went quiet × ×1,5 if still advertising). A worklist has
its own ranking, and a ranking cannot live as a column in an alphabetical
company table.

The tab exists because of one measurement: **only 6,2 % of lost Verkaufschancen
go to a competitor.** The biggest single loss reason is *Kein Feedback vom
Kunden* (9.078 cases), and with *Kein Interesse mehr* (3.391) roughly a third of
all losses is lost to **silence**, not to a rival.

### 3.4 Objekte — the project view

**Data:** `crm_opportunities` (57.776, years 2023–2026) grouped by
`sl_primary_opportunityid`, plus `crm_opportunity_products` for the mix.

An **Objekt has no record of its own in CRM.** It is a *group* of Verkaufschancen
that share a primary id — several dealers bidding on one building. The tab
assembles it. **One win makes the whole project won**; the siblings are closed as
*"Zugehörige VC gewonnen"* (a CRM value, not our invention) and counting those as
losses overstates failure.

That is why there are **52.796 Objekte but only 48.239 Firmen**: 49.216 Objekte
hold a single Verkaufschance (the primary id points at the deal itself when it
stands alone), and one company can be on many buildings.

```
Vertriebswege:  Fachhandelsvertrieb 47.070 · Objektvertrieb 3.987
                Architektenberatung 3.575 · Direktvertrieb 2.890 · SLect 224
Herkunft:       vom Händler 27.912 · von Solarlux 24.500 · vom Architekten 2.637
                aus Online Konfigurator 2.000 · vom Objektkunden 529
Status:         verloren 38.571 · offen 11.521 · gewonnen 7.684
```

**The filter added today** — Verkaufschancen je Objekt — because the count is the
strongest project-level signal measured so far, and it is monotonic:

| VCs am Objekt | Gewinnrate | Objekte |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 18,1 % | 49.216 |
| 2 | 35,5 % | 2.805 |
| 3–4 | 48,4 % | 626 |
| 5–9 | 68,1 % | 145 |
| 10+ | 100,0 % | 4 *(noise)* |

Clicking an Objekt opens everything ever linked to it: every member VC, every
firm in every role (Käufer / Architekt / Endkunde) with what it did, the product
mix specified on the site, the SAP orders, and a dated timeline.

---

### 3.5 Prüfen — the human decision queue

**47 items today** (38 `needs_review` + 9 `conflict`). These are website
candidates that are *plausible but not proven*: the automatic check accepts only
hard evidence, and everything else lands here with the deciding hint attached so
one click is enough.

It is separate because it is not information — it is **work**. Mixing a decision
queue into a data table means the decisions never get made.

---

### 3.6 ICP — Ideales Kundenprofil

**Data:** none of its own. It reads `companies` and writes `fit_score`,
`target_score`, `fit_breakdown` back onto them. 10 profiles saved so far.

Deliberately **counting, not machine learning**: for each feature it stores the
winners' value distribution and scores a company by how typical its values are,
normalised so the modal winner value = 1,0. Every score decomposes into a German
one-liner a BD colleague can read. No black box, or nobody trusts the ranking.

```
Gewichte:  Segment 2,0 · Produkte 2,0 · Untersegment 1,5 · Vertriebsweg 1,0
           PLZ-Zone 1,0 · Betriebsgröße 1,0 · Anzeigen-Aktivität 1,0 · Firmenalter 0,5
```

Two rules that were learned the hard way and are enforced in tests:

- **Private Endkunden are excluded from the winners set.** 332 of them had
  bought, which silently made them 32 % of the default winners and dragged a
  *trade partner* profile toward consumer traits.
- **Missing data never punishes.** A feature the company has no value for is
  excluded and the remaining weights renormalised.

The other two scores: `opportunity_score` is the Divergenz (is there something to
win *now*), `target_score` is the geometric mean of fit and opportunity — the
ranked call list.

## 4. How this feeds an ICP / IPP — and what still blocks it

**Feature sources**

| from | features |
|---|---|
| CRM master data | Segment, Untersegment, Vertriebsweg, PLZ-Zone, Land, KV |
| CRM products | which families a firm deals in (`crm_company_products`) |
| CRM projects | Vertriebsweg, Herkunft, Nutzungsart, **VCs je Objekt** |
| Website enrichment | Betriebsgröße, Alter, Eigenfertigung, Showroom, Zertifikate, Wettbewerbsmarken, Positionierung, Projektfokus, `solarlux_fit` |
| AdWatch ads | Anzeigen-Aktivität |

**Label sources**

- ICP (company): has a material order (≥ 2.000 €) — 3.792 firms, **8,1 %** base rate
- IPP (project): the Objekt was won — 8.189 of 52.796

**Four things that block a trustworthy ranking right now:**

1. **Architekten cannot be scored by purchase.** 0,1 % buy rate on 44 % of the
   base. Their label has to be "specified into a won project", which means the
   Objekte→Architekt link is the ICP work, not the company table.
2. **Availability leakage.** `size_bucket` comes from enrichment, and enrichment
   was run on the monitored base — which *was* the buyers list. `ad_presence`
   exists only for companies with a linked ad page, chosen from the same base.
   Both then "predict" buying while carrying no information about fit. The CRM
   equivalent, `numberofemployees` (filled on 467 of 15.235 dealers, buy rate
   47–75 % where filled vs 20,7 % where not, because reps fill it in for accounts
   they already work), is deliberately **not imported** and tests assert it stays out.
3. **Enrichment covers 1.447 of 48.239 companies (3 %).** Every website-derived
   feature is missing for 97 % of the base.
4. **No holdout, no outcome tracking.** Nothing yet measures whether a high score
   actually predicted anything (open task #29).

**Two confounds on the VC-count signal** before treating it as causal: bigger
buildings may simply attract more bidders (size, not the count), and Solarlux may
register sibling VCs preferentially on deals it already expects to win
(recording bias, which would make it circular).

## 6. Vocabulary — mine vs. Solarlux's

Two words in this app were invented by Claude, not by Solarlux or the CRM. Both
are now removed, and the pattern is worth watching for:

- **"Sammelprojekt"** — plausible German for "Objekt with several VCs", which is
  what made it dangerous: a colleague would have gone looking for it in Dynamics.
  Replaced with *"Objekte mit mehreren VCs"*.
- **"Chancen"** as a tab name read as CRM Verkaufschancen when it means silent
  customers. Renamed to *Rückgewinnung*, with an explicit line saying these are
  **not** Verkaufschancen.

Real CRM terms that must stay exact: *Verkaufschance*, *Objektvertrieb*,
*Zugehörige VC gewonnen*, *Beleg*, *Kundensegment*, *Vertriebsweg*, *KV*.
